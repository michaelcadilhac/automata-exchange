A series \(f : \Sigma^* \to \mathbb Q\) is commutative if the output does not depend on the order of the symbols in the input.
Given a polynomial automaton recognising a commutative series,
does there exist an equivalent polynomial automaton where all control states recognise commutative series?
The answer for weighted automata is yes.
